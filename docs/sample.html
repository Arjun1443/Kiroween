Night Watch: Urban Moths

This fictional field note is included with Tombstone to demonstrate how an archived page becomes a cited research card.

On an April evening, volunteers recorded moth sightings at three sites in a neighborhood park. They noted the time, weather, and nearby light sources for each observation.

The team recorded the most sightings near a row of flowering shrubs. The notes describe observations from one evening and cannot establish whether the shrubs caused the difference.

The volunteers planned a second survey with equal observation times at each site and a consistent recording sheet.
